Your keypair

Generate a keypair to receive secrets and to sign the secrets you send, so recipients can verify they really came from you. It's encrypted with a passphrase you choose and stored only in this browser's local storage — it is never transmitted anywhere.

Your keypair predates message signing and can't sign outgoing secrets yet. Upgrading keeps your existing key (secrets already sent to you will still decrypt) and adds a signing key. Note: your fingerprint will change, since it now covers both keys — any contacts who pinned your old fingerprint will need to re-verify you once.

Trusted contacts

The first link from someone is only as trustworthy as the channel it arrived on — verify their fingerprint with them another way (in person, a call, a separate app) before saving it here. Once saved, links carrying that same key are recognized automatically, and you can send them a new secret anytime without repeating the link exchange. If a future link claims to be them but carries a different key, you'll be warned before it can overwrite the saved one.

Security & self-hosting notes

This page shares browser storage with every other WebUtils app on this domain. That's not a problem for the public-key material this app stores, but for stronger isolation you can download this file (see "Download" next to Secret Share on the WebUtils index) and host it on its own domain or subdomain. A separate origin means no other app's code can ever read this app's local storage, no matter what happens to it later.
